About

I'm a platform engineer. By day I run Kubernetes and OpenShift at large scale in a regulated bank, and the LLM inference infrastructure on top of it. On my own time I work on the open-source projects that serve LLMs on Kubernetes. For two years I was also the founding engineer of a stablecoin whose treasury grew past $30M.

What I work on

Clusters many teams share. Kubernetes and OpenShift at large scale, under banking regulation. Changes ship through GitOps, and the platform has to stay boring for the teams that build on it.

LLM inference as a platform. Serving a model to one team is easy. Serving it to every team on the same GPUs takes pooling, scheduling and quotas. That's the part of the stack I contribute to.

Knowing what's running. Alerting and recording rules, custom exporters, dashboards. On a shared platform you have to see who used what before you can promise anything.
